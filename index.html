The Real Estate IRA

Leverage your retirement funds to invest directly in physical real estate through a Self-Directed IRA

Key Rules & Restrictions

  • You cannot invest in property you already own
  • Avoid prohibited transactions with disqualified persons (family members, etc.)
  • Your IRA, not you personally, must own the property
  • All expenses and income must flow through your IRA
  • You cannot personally use the property
  • Work with qualified professionals to avoid tax issues

Investment Process

1
Set up a Self-Directed IRA
Choose a custodian that specializes in real estate investments
2
Fund Your Account
Contribute, transfer from another IRA, or roll over a 401(k)
3
Find Your Investment Property
Locate a suitable investment that meets your criteria
4
Complete Documentation
Your IRA custodian signs all documents (not you personally)
5
Close on the Property
Your IRA completes the purchase
6
Manage the Investment
All expenses and income must flow through your IRA
