Professional Biography

Rodrigo Munguia brings an innovative world view to the Residential Brokerage Division of D&R Real Estate. His consistent and persevering methods have proven to be the formula for success in the Residential Real Estate and Commercial Development arenas.

With over 30 years of experience in project management, construction, and real estate development, Rodrigo has established himself as a versatile professional capable of managing complex projects from conception to completion. His expertise spans across various sectors including high-end residential construction, landscape architecture, material handling systems, and commercial real estate development.

As the owner and operator of D & R Consulting, LLC, Rodrigo specializes in commercial real estate due diligence services, construction loan consulting, project development, spec home construction, project management, financial analysis, and land acquisitions. He serves local clients as well as investors from Latin America, Mexico, Japan, and Spain, representing them in both brokerage and property development capacities.

Rodrigo's multilingual capabilities (fluent in English and Spanish) and his deep understanding of international business practices have enabled him to build a diverse client portfolio and successfully navigate complex cross-cultural negotiations. His technical background in Construction Engineering, combined with his extensive certifications in safety standards and specialized training, makes him a valuable asset in any real estate or construction project.

Throughout his career, Rodrigo has demonstrated exceptional leadership skills, managing teams across multiple disciplines and ensuring the successful delivery of projects ranging from $300,000 to $6,700,000 in value. His commitment to quality, attention to detail, and client-focused approach have earned him a reputation for excellence in the industry.
